WEBSITE PRIVACY.

Last reviewed: 12 October 2026

This page describes the privacy practices of the Eddy Studios informational website hosted at adnilsonbarbosa.github.io/eddy-studios-website. This notice does not replace the separate privacy policies for any of our games.

What this website collects

We do not currently operate contact forms, user accounts, advertising trackers or our own website analytics on this site. This website does not intentionally request personal information from visitors.

Hosting

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may automatically process technical data, such as IP addresses or request logs, to operate and secure its services. Please refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Links to third parties

Our site includes links to third-party services, including Google Play and GitHub. Their privacy practices and terms are separate from ours.

Games and future changes

Individual games may have different data practices; consult the privacy policy linked from each game's store listing. If we add forms, analytics or other services to this website, we will update this notice.

Questions

For studio information, visit our developer profile. A dedicated business contact channel may be added later.
